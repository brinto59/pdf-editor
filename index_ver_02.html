PDF Toolkit

Merge, edit, sign and compress PDFs, or turn scans and photos into PDFs. Everything stays on your device.

On phones this opens the camera. Elsewhere it opens a file picker.

Signature (optional)

Upload a photo or scan of your signature. Crop, rotate, straighten and correct perspective, then the background is removed automatically. Use ✍ on a page under “Edit pages” to place it.

    PDF structure is always optimized. Compression also recompresses embedded JPEG images; text and vectors are untouched.

    Runs entirely client-side with pdf-lib and pdf.js.
